              For GIS software Flood depth (GeoTIFF)

              The GeoTIFF holds water depth in metres for every 30 m cell. Open it in QGIS or ArcGIS.

              A quick estimate from global satellite data. It shows where flooding is likely, not an engineering flood study, and it can miss walls, culverts and drains. Local knowledge matters: check the map against what your community has seen in past floods.
